# What to ask

Starter phrases from the skills. A skill starts by itself when the request fits, so there is no need to name it.

## Build and share

**[Build a survey from a brief](../skills/surveyninja-survey-from-brief/)**

- “Build a survey for our repair shop customers: rate the work and collect contacts for a follow-up visit”
- “I need a short satisfaction survey after delivery: three questions and an email”
- “Create a consultation request form with name, phone, preferred time and the customer's question”

**[Skip logic and branching](../skills/surveyninja-survey-logic/)**

- “If someone rates us below four, ask them why”
- “Skip the quality questions for people who never used the service”
- “Check the survey logic for dead ends”

**[Rewrite survey copy](../skills/surveyninja-survey-copy/)**

- “Rewrite the questions in this survey in plain language”
- “People quit at question three, look at the wording”
- “Make the first screen of the survey worth reading”

**[Distribute the survey](../skills/surveyninja-survey-distribution/)**

- “How do I send this survey to several channels and tell them apart later?”
- “Make a QR code for the survey to put on the counter”
- “I need a private survey with passwords for twenty people”

**[Launch kit](../skills/surveyninja-launch-kit/)**

- “Prepare everything for the launch: links, copy and a QR code”
- “Write the survey announcement for our newsletter and for WhatsApp”
- “We are launching in three channels, make a labeled link for each”

## Check before launch

**[Pre-launch check](../skills/surveyninja-prelaunch-check/)**

- “Check my survey before launch. Is everything ready?”
- “I am about to send the link. Did I forget anything?”
- “Find logic and text problems before I publish”

**[Data quality check](../skills/surveyninja-data-quality/)**

- “Can I trust these responses? Check the data quality”
- “This survey looks gamed. Take a look at the data”
- “Find suspicious completions and tell me if they change the conclusions”

## Understand results

**[Survey results summary](../skills/surveyninja-results-digest/)**

- “What did the survey show? Give me the short version”
- “Show results for September only, and only for people who came from the link I emailed”
- “Summarize the survey and tell me what deserves attention”

**[Analyze open-ended answers](../skills/surveyninja-open-answers/)**

- “Group the free-text answers into themes and show what comes up most”
- “What are people writing in the “what could be better” question?”
- “Pull themes out of the open answers with quotes”

**[Completion funnel](../skills/surveyninja-response-funnel/)**

- “Why does this survey get so few responses? Find where people quit”
- “How many people finish the survey and where do they drop off?”
- “Break down the completion funnel and suggest what to fix”

**[A/B variant analysis](../skills/surveyninja-ab-significance/)**

- “Compare the A/B variants in this survey. Which one is better?”
- “Can we pick a winner yet, or is the data too thin?”
- “Check whether the difference between the two question wordings is significant”

**[Comparing with past results](../skills/surveyninja-benchmark/)**

- “Compare the autumn wave of the survey with the spring one”
- “Did things get better or worse since the last measurement?”
- “Compare completion rates across two surveys in my account”

**[How many responses you need](../skills/surveyninja-sample-size/)**

- “Can we draw conclusions yet or should we keep collecting?”
- “How many responses do I need to compare two cities?”
- “Is there enough data to make a decision from this survey?”

**[Survey timeline](../skills/surveyninja-survey-timeline/)**

- “Why did the survey stop collecting responses?”
- “Show how responses came in and when we edited the survey”
- “There was a drop in late August. Work out what it was tied to”

**[Comparing traffic sources](../skills/surveyninja-source-dashboard/)**

- “Where did respondents come from and which channel worked best?”
- “Compare the email campaign and Instagram by response quality”
- “Split the responses by source label”

**[Results for the client](../skills/surveyninja-results-sharing/)**

- “Send the client the survey results for September”
- “Make a report link I can show my manager”
- “Build a report with conclusions and share it as a link”

**[Report and survey styling](../skills/surveyninja-report-appearance/)**

- “Give the survey a theme in our brand colors”
- “Change the chart colors in this survey's report”
- “Put the company logo on our surveys and reports”

- “Set up a webhook to send responses to our server”
- “Send the phone from the survey to our CRM as a field called phone”

- “Responses stopped arriving in our Google Sheet. Find out why”
- “Telegram notifications stopped coming. What happened?”
- “Check every integration on this survey and resend what piled up”

**[Lead quality](../skills/surveyninja-lead-quality/)**

- “How many usable leads did this survey collect?”
- “Check the quality of our leads and tell me where we lose them”
- “Which channel brings better leads?”
